## Profile vector decorations

Original SVG typography, icons and layouts in `profile/` use a MyGO-inspired palette: blue #3388BB, light blue #77BBDD, pink #FF8899, green #77DD77, yellow #FFDD88 and purple #7777AA. Darker shades improve text readability.

X icon: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/x.svg (Simple Icons, CC0). Technology badges: Shields.io, downloaded as SVGs with this palette.
